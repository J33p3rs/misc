# Useful AI Risk and Governance Links

This page provides curated links to useful resources for AI risk assessment, governance, and responsible AI practices.

---

## Reference Table

| Area | Source | Link | Comments |
|------|--------|------|----------|
| AI Risk Assessment | MIT AI Risk Repository | https://airisk.mit.edu/ | Detailed repository of risks totalling over 1700 items. |
| AI Risk Assessment | EqualAI – Algorithmic Impact Assessment Template | https://www.equalai.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/EqualAI_Algorithmic-Impact-Assessment-Template.pdf | Practical AIA template aligned with NIST AI RMF. |
| AI Impact Assessment | Microsoft – Responsible AI Tools and Practices | https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/ai/tools-practices | Collection of responsible AI guidance, frameworks, and tools for enterprise use. |
| AI Impact Assessment | Australian Government – AI Impact Assessment Tool | https://www.digital.gov.au/ai/impact-assessment-tool | Official Australian template aligned with national AI ethics principles. |
| Regulation | EU AI Act Overview | https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/ | Central resource for understanding EU AI Act requirements and obligations. Includes checker: https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/assessment/eu-ai-act-compliance-checker/ |
| Standards | NIST AI Risk Management Framework | https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework | Foundational framework for managing AI risks across lifecycle stages. |
| Standards | ISO/IEC 42001 (AI Management Systems) | https://www.iso.org/standard/81230.html | International standard for AI management systems and governance. |
| Security | OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications | https://owasp.org/www-project-top-10-for-large-language-model-applications/ | Key security risks specific to LLM and generative AI systems. |
| AI Security Risk Pattern | Simon Willison – The Lethal Trifecta for AI Agents | https://simonw.substack.com/p/the-lethal-trifecta-for-ai-agents | Describes the high-risk combination of data access, untrusted input, and external actions in AI agents. |
