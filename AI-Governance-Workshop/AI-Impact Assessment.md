# AI Use Case Assessment
## Opportunity

- Document the opportunity for the use case  
- What are the expected business benefits?  
- What is the overall value proposition?  
- How will you measure success?  
- What is the estimated effort and time to prototype the idea?  

## Alignment with Org Goals

- Mission, Vision, and Values  

## Key Risk Areas

- Lethal trifecta  https://simonw.substack.com/p/the-lethal-trifecta-for-ai-agents

## Stakeholder Impact

- List all stakeholders, including societies, individuals, and groups of individuals  

## Review

### Actions
- Tasks to mitigate risks identified in the process  
- May include full impact assessment  

## ISMS Checklist

- Asset Management (information assets and infrastructure, etc.)  
- Suppliers  
- Access Control  
- Risk Management – do we need to add risks to the register?  
- Business Continuity, backup, redundancy  
- Testing and oversight  
- Vulnerability management and security testing  
- Documentation updates
